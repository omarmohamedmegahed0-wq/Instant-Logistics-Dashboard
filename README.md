# 🚛 Instant Logistics Operational & Financial Dashboard (Excel)

## 📌 Project Overview
This project presents a comprehensive **Logistics & Supply Chain Analytics Dashboard** built in Microsoft Excel. The primary objective is to evaluate fleet operations, optimize operating expenses, monitor customer contracts, and track driver performance across thousands of shipments for **Instant Logistics**.

## 📊 Key Insights & Highlights

- **Financial Performance:**
  - **Total Revenue:** $262.5M across **76,939 completed trips**.
  - **Net Profit:** $168.3M achieving a strong **64.1% Profit Margin**.
  - **Total Weight Delivered:** Over **2.34 Billion lbs/kg** of freight transported.

- **Operational Cost Analysis (Total Expenses: $94.2M):**
  - **Fuel Costs:** $66.5M (Largest operational expense line).
  - **Driver Charges:** $22.0M.
  - **Fleet Maintenance:** $5.7M (Broken down into Preventive, Repairs, Tires, Brakes, Transmission, and Inspections).

- **Customer & Freight Breakdown:**
  - Analyzed 200 total customers across Contract, Dedicated, and Spot market agreements.
  - **Top Freight Categories:** Automotive (~$54.7M), Food/Beverage (~$48.5M), Retail (~$46.1M), and Electronics (~$42.8M).

- **Fleet & Driver Performance Tracking:**
  - Evaluated **124 active drivers** on key performance indicators:
    - **On-Time Delivery Rate:** Network average of ~55.7%.
    - **Fuel Economy:** Tracked Average Miles Per Gallon (MPG ~6.5 - 7.0).
    - **Idle Time Efficiency:** Analyzed average idle hours (~7.0 hrs) to reduce unnecessary fuel consumption.
